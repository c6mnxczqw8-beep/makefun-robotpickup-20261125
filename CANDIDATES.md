# Robot pickup candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 200 characters: the funniest pickup
line a robot could use.

1. Software update: "Are you a software update? Because I've
   been avoiding commitment, but you make me want to restart
   and change." -- romantic and true to the medium.
2. Background process: "Excuse me, are you running a
   background process? Because you've been on my mind using
   100% of my CPU since you walked in." -- the CPU flattery
   is total; a robot's highest compliment, and the winner's
   shape.
3. Charging: "My battery lasts nine hours, but I'd spend
   eight of them standing here pretending not to watch you
   charge." -- wistful robot longing.
4. CAPTCHA: "I solved nine CAPTCHAs to get into this bar,
   and I'd solve nine more just to be told you're not a
   robot either." -- effort as courtship.
5. Error 404: "I ran a full diagnostic and found one error:
   your number, not found." -- the classic, polished.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. A robot's flattery should be measurable,
and 100% CPU is the maximum a robot can honestly offer --
total occupation, no background tasks, since the moment of
walking in. Candidate 1 is sweeter; candidate 2 commits more
hardware, which for a robot is the more serious vow.
